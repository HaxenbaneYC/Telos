The 13 weeks
The rule
Real food, cooked by you. Eggs, chicken thigh and breast, salmon, steak, Greek yogurt, cheese, milk, potato, rice, oats, fruit, olive oil, nuts. Five eggs is fine — dietary cholesterol doesn't meaningfully move blood cholesterol in healthy young men. Skip the processed middle: cream, bottled dressings, packaged sauces, protein bars.

The week
Push and Pull are Nippard. Upper and Legs are your boxing plan. Saturday is new — your two plans give about 5 lateral and 2 bicep sets a week, which won't build the shoulders and arms you want.
Sessions
Back from 6 weeks off
Weeks 1–2 at 3–4 reps in reserve, starting near 60% of your old loads. Strength returns in weeks; tendons take longer, and that gap is how people get hurt in week two. Don't chase soreness. The upside: regaining lost muscle is far faster than building new, so you can drop fat and regain size at once.

No loaded side bends or weighted obliques. A thicker waist fights the V.
